# Living Themes
Each theme: **thesis → evidence (dated, sourced, append-only) → how to talk about it in an interview**. Revisions to a thesis are added as dated notes, never overwritten.

---

## 1. Higher-for-longer is back: the Fed is hiking into an oil shock
**Thesis (2026-10-03):** The 2024–25 easing cycle reversed. The Warsh Fed hiked in September, and the 10y is above 5% for the first time since the early 2000s. That compresses LBO math (debt costs higher, exit multiples lower) and favors strategic buyers paying in stock and credit-heavy alt managers.
**Evidence**
- 2026-09-16: Fed +25bp to 3.75–4.00%, first hike since Jul 2023; dots imply one more ([fedratecalc](https://fedratecalc.com/fomc-meeting-schedule/september-2026/))
- 2026-08-28: Warsh at Jackson Hole says inflation is "too high" ([NPR](https://npr.org/2026/08/28/nx-s1-5947903/federal-reserve-inflation-jackson-hole-interest-rates))
- Q3 2026: 10y hit 5.34%, highest since 2002 ([GB&F/Reuters](https://www.globalbankingandfinance.com/global-m-deal-rush-fades-third-quarter-rising-borrowing/))
- 2026-10-02: payrolls +29k vs 84k; Oct hike odds 64% → 22% ([Yahoo](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/p-500-nasdaq-dow-end-222859024.html))
**How to talk about it:** "The interesting tension right now is a Fed that's hiking because of an energy-driven inflation impulse while the labor market is visibly cracking. For deals, that means sponsors face 5%+ base rates plus spread, so you're seeing more stock-for-stock strategic mergers and more take-privates of discounted REITs than classic levered buyouts."

## 2. M&A: record year, but the Q3 air-pocket
**Thesis:** 2026 YTD value is the best since 2001, driven by megadeals and AI. But Q3 fell 41% q/q as rates spiked. Fees lag announcements, so banks still print strong 2H revenue.
**Evidence**
- Q3 $993bn (−41%); YTD $3.9tn (+28%); only 10 $10bn+ deals ([GB&F/Reuters](https://www.globalbankingandfinance.com/global-m-deal-rush-fades-third-quarter-rising-borrowing/))
- Sep 15 cluster of strategic deals: GE Aerospace–CPP $11.75bn (vertical integration, ~$7bn cash plus new debt); Cegid–Silae €10bn all-stock; IRT–Centerspace $8.1bn EV all-stock; Tamarack–Headwater C$10bn all-stock; EverBank–WaFd $3.9bn ([Intellizence](https://intellizence.com/insights/merger-and-acquisition/top-5-ma-deals-of-the-week-reshaping-aerospace-software-real-estate-energy-and-banking/)). **Note how many are all-stock**, which is what you'd expect when cash and debt are expensive.
**How to talk about it:** lead with the all-stock pattern; it's a sharp observation that links deal structure to rates.

## 3. Public-to-private real estate arbitrage
**Thesis:** Higher rates widened REIT discounts to NAV. Private capital (Blackstone, Ares, Brookfield) buys listed portfolios cheaper than the same assets trade privately.
**Evidence**
- 2026-08-13: BX RE / Crestpoint / PSP to acquire H&R REIT, C$6.7bn ([STB](https://www.stblaw.com/about-us/news/view/2026/08/13/h-r-reit-to-be-acquired-in-c$6.7-billion-transaction))
- 2026-04: Ares to acquire Whitestone REIT, $1.7bn, $19.00/sh cash ([Kirkland](https://www.kirkland.com/news/press-release/2026/04/kirkland-advises-ares-on-1-7-billion-acquisition-of-whitestone-reit))
- 2026: Brookfield to acquire Peakstone Realty, ~$1.2bn, $21/sh ([MarketScreener](https://ca.marketscreener.com/news/brookfield-to-acquire-peakstone-realty-trust-for-1-2bn-ce7e5bd2d98ff725))
- 2026-09-15: IRT–Centerspace $8.1bn all-stock multifamily merger. Public REITs consolidating among themselves is the other response to the discount ([Intellizence](https://intellizence.com/insights/merger-and-acquisition/top-5-ma-deals-of-the-week-reshaping-aerospace-software-real-estate-energy-and-banking/))
- 2026-09-28: Warburg Pincus raised its Ingenia Communities offer to ~$1.5bn ([PE Wire](https://www.privateequitywire.co.uk/content_channels/deals/))
**How to talk about it:** "Take-private when the public market discount exceeds what you'd pay privately. The risk is the denominator: if the 10y holds above 5%, exit cap rates have to widen too."

## 4. Private credit under scrutiny: redemptions in the wealth channel
**Evidence**
- BX kept its 5% quarterly withdrawal cap on a ~$77bn credit vehicle; KKR K-Income requests ~2.5% of NAV in Q3 ([Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/equities/26/09/62012444/apollo-global-blue-owl-and-blackstone-shares-slide-even-as-private-credit-fears-fade))
- 2026-10: Blue Owl investors sought to pull 39% of a $5bn tech credit fund ([Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/private-markets/26/10/62146487/blue-owl-investors-seek-to-pull-39-from-5b-tech-credit-fund))
**How to talk about it:** separate the *asset* question (credit quality, which has been fine so far) from the *liability* question (semi-liquid retail vehicles promising quarterly liquidity on illiquid loans). The second is the real stress point.
