# Blackstone
**Type:** Alt manager: PE / RE / credit · **Ticker:** BX · **Last updated:** 2026-10-03

## Snapshot
World's largest alternative manager; largest commercial-real-estate owner; BCRED / private credit; BXPE, infrastructure (BXINFRA), data centers (QTS).

## Ready answer: "Tell me about a recent deal"
**H&R REIT take-private (announced Aug 13, 2026, ~C$6.7bn)** — consortium of Blackstone Real Estate, Crestpoint and PSP acquiring all of H&R REIT's assets in a cash-and-equity deal, closing expected Q4 2026 [src](https://www.stblaw.com/about-us/news/view/2026/08/13/h-r-reit-to-be-acquired-in-c$6.7-billion-transaction). Rationale: public REITs trading at discounts to NAV let private capital buy real estate cheaper than in the private market — classic Blackstone 'public-to-private arbitrage'. Why now: higher rates widened REIT discounts. Bear case: if the 10y stays ~5%+, cap rates may need to rise further, eroding the entry discount. *Cap rate / premium: n/d — first run to fill.*

## Macro lens
Higher-for-longer cuts both ways: it pressures RE values (bear for existing book) but creates take-private discounts and lifts private-credit yields (bull for new deployment). Retail-vehicle redemptions (BCRED/BREIT) are the risk to watch.
