## The regime in one paragraph
The Fed under Chair **Kevin Warsh** is **hiking, not cutting**: +25bp on Sep 16, 2026 to **3.75–4.00%**, its first hike since July 2023, with the median dot implying one more by year-end ([fedratecalc](https://fedratecalc.com/fomc-meeting-schedule/september-2026/), secondary source; effective fed funds 3.88% per [Fed H.15](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/h15/) confirms the range). Inflation is running hot (CPI 3.4% y/y and the Fed's preferred gauge 3.7% y/y through July ([NPR](https://npr.org/2026/08/28/nx-s1-5947903/federal-reserve-inflation-jackson-hole-interest-rates))), helped by an oil shock from the **US–Iran conflict**. Long rates are at **multi-decade highs**: the 10y reached 5.34%, its highest since 2002, during Q3 ([Reuters/LSEG via GB&F](https://www.globalbankingandfinance.com/global-m-deal-rush-fades-third-quarter-rising-borrowing/)). Then **September payrolls missed badly** (+29k vs 84k est.; unemployment 4.2%), and October hike odds fell to ~22% from 64% a week earlier ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/p-500-nasdaq-dow-end-222859024.html)). In short, stagflation-lite: hot prices, a cooling labor market, and a Fed boxed in.
